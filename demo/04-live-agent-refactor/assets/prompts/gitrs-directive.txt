Directive for this session: you have the `gitrs` skill for Rust refactoring (patch-first: read, author, check, apply). Whenever a task involves editing Rust code, prefer gitrs semantic hunks over hand-edits, and consult the skill for patch shapes, directives, and verification. The `gitrs`, `applyrs`, and `diffrs` binaries are on your PATH; their workspace-relative directory is `../../../target/release/`.
Work in as few patches as possible: compose the whole refactor as ONE patch (every file, every hunk), validate it with a single `gitrs apply --check`, grep the tree to confirm `matches` covers every site, then apply once. Trust the skill's complete examples for standard shapes (rename, sweep, move); probe only genuinely novel shapes, and never apply sub-task by sub-task.
